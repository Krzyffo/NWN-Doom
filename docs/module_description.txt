NWN-DOOM

Neverwinter Nights played like Doom: first-person, fast, and click-to-shoot. Survive endless waves of demons and the undead, grab what they drop, and keep moving.

Bring any character of level 1-5. Monster damage scales with your hit points, so every level plays the same.

--- FIRST TIME ---
Put the weapons on F1, F2 and F3: open your inventory and drag the items "1: Fist", "2: Pistol" and "3: Shotgun" onto those quickbar slots. Faster: make quickbar text macros that say /1, /2 and /3 instead; they switch weapons instantly.

--- CONTROLS ---
Move: W / S forward and back, A / D turn (or strafe, depending on your key setup). The camera always looks where you face.
Shoot: click anywhere. You fire straight ahead at the crosshair in the middle of the screen, so turn to aim. While your gun is out the mouse pointer is a small red dot: keep it on the ground or on a monster, because clicks on the sky do not register.
Switch weapons: F1 Fist, F2 Pistol, F3 Shotgun.
Open doors: click a door when you are standing next to it. Locked doors need their key.
Pick up items: walk over them. Ammo, stimpacks and armor drop from dead monsters.

If right-click or Escape put your gun away, type /gun in chat to get it back.

--- TIPS ---
Turning feels slow or stiff? Raise the camera turn speed in Options > Controls.
Imps throw slow fireballs. Strafe sideways to dodge them.
The shotgun drops from a kill once you reach wave 2. Its pellets spread, so it hits hardest up close and can catch several monsters at once.
Armor soaks a third of the damage you take.
A balor sometimes joins from wave 3. It is slow but very tough. Keep your distance.
If you die, you are back at the start a few seconds later with a pistol.

--- CHAT COMMANDS ---
/1 /2 /3 - fist, pistol, shotgun (same as F1-F3)
/gun - get your gun back out
/speed 0-50 - how much faster than normal you run (default 50, the most the game allows)
/fps - switch between first and third person
/lockcam - lock or unlock the camera to your facing
/hud - redraw the status bar (after changing resolution)
/wave - call in the next wave now
/idkfa - all weapons, full ammo and armor (cheat)
/balor - summon a balor (testing)

Requires the nwn_doom hak (Freedoom art).
